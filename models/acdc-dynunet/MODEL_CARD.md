# Model card: acdc-dynunet

## Summary
ACDC cardiac MRI segmentation (DynUNet). Predicts right ventricle cavity, myocardium and left ventricle cavity from short-axis cine cardiac MRI, end-diastole and end-systole frames. No run has been published yet.

## Intended use
Research only. Not a medical device. Do not use it to diagnose, plan treatment or measure
patients.

## Data
- ACDC (MICCAI 2017), 150 patients, from the official Girder collection (public, no account). Download: `uv run python scripts/datasets/acdc.py data/acdc` writes `data/acdc/manifest.csv`.
  Download size: 176 MB (ED and ES frames and masks only; the 2.3 GB of 4D cine volumes are skipped).
- License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, for non-commercial scientific research only; citing Bernard et al. 2018 is mandatory.
- Split: Official: the 50 testing patients (masks now public) are the test split; the 100 training patients are split 80/20 into train and validation, stratified by diagnosis group. Both frames of a patient stay together.
- Labels: Manual, 0 background, 1 RV, 2 myocardium, 3 LV (Bernard et al. 2018).

## Training
Config: `config.yaml`, generated by `scripts/segmentation_recipes.py`. MONAI DynUNet, Dice
plus cross-entropy, SGD (Nesterov, momentum 0.99) with polynomial decay, bf16 mixed precision.
Volumes are resampled to 1.5 x 1.5 x 5 mm. Training takes 4 patches of 192 x 192 x 16
voxels per step from 2 volumes, for 625 epochs. Inference is Gaussian-weighted sliding window with
half-patch overlap, and scoring happens on each label's original grid.

Estimated training memory at this patch and batch: 4.5 GiB (bytes autograd keeps
for backward under bf16 autocast plus fp32 weights, gradients and optimizer state, measured
on CPU; CUDA workspace and allocator overhead come on top). It is meant to fit a 12 GB GPU;
this has not been checked on one.

## Results
None yet. The headline is mean Dice over classes with a
95% patient-level bootstrap interval, from the run's `metrics.json`; per-case Dice and HD95
are in `predictions_test.csv`.

Published context: nnU-Net reached mean Dice 0.9295 on the official test set (baselines.yaml). See `baselines.yaml` for what is comparable.

## Limitations and biases
Two scanners at one centre; 5 to 10 mm slices.

## Reproduce
```bash
uv run python scripts/datasets/acdc.py data/acdc
uv run opendiagnostics fit -c models/acdc-dynunet/config.yaml
```
