# Model card: picai-dynunet

## Summary
PI-CAI csPCa lesion segmentation (DynUNet). Predicts clinically significant prostate cancer lesions (ISUP 2 or higher) from biparametric prostate MRI: T2W, ADC and high-b-value DWI. No run has been published yet.

## Intended use
Research only. Not a medical device. Do not use it to diagnose, plan treatment or measure
patients.

## Data
- PI-CAI public training and development set, 1,500 studies from 1,476 patients (https://zenodo.org/records/6624726) with masks from picai_labels. Download: `uv run --with SimpleITK python scripts/datasets/picai.py data/picai` writes `data/picai/manifest.csv`.
  Download size: 26.9 GB in five zips plus the picai_labels repository.
- License: CC BY-NC 4.0. Non-commercial use; weights and predictions may be shared with attribution.
- Split: The hidden test set's labels are not public, so studies are split 70/10/20 by patient (some patients have two studies), stratified by case_csPCa, seed 0.
- Labels: Human expert masks for all 1,500 studies: the original 1,295 and Pooch et al. 2025's 205; graded masks made binary.

## Training
Config: `config.yaml`, generated by `scripts/segmentation_recipes.py`. MONAI DynUNet, Dice
plus cross-entropy, SGD (Nesterov, momentum 0.99) with polynomial decay, bf16 mixed precision.
Volumes are resampled to 0.5 x 0.5 x 3 mm. Training takes 2 patches of 256 x 256 x 20
voxels per step from 1 volume, for 48 epochs. Inference is Gaussian-weighted sliding window with
half-patch overlap, and scoring happens on each label's original grid.

Estimated training memory at this patch and batch: 6.0 GiB (bytes autograd keeps
for backward under bf16 autocast plus fp32 weights, gradients and optimizer state, measured
on CPU; CUDA workspace and allocator overhead come on top). It is meant to fit a 12 GB GPU;
this has not been checked on one.

## Results
None yet. The headline is mean Dice over classes with a
95% patient-level bootstrap interval, from the run's `metrics.json`; per-case Dice and HD95
are in `predictions_test.csv`.

Published context: No published lesion Dice under a reproducible protocol was found. See `baselines.yaml` for what is comparable.

## Limitations and biases
Dice is averaged over studies with a lesion, so false positives on the roughly 70% negative studies are not counted. PI-CAI ranks by AUROC and average precision, which this pipeline does not compute for segmentation. Includes 328 ProstateX cases; do not add ProstateX.

## Reproduce
```bash
uv run --with SimpleITK python scripts/datasets/picai.py data/picai
uv run opendiagnostics fit -c models/picai-dynunet/config.yaml
```
