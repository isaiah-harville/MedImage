# Model card: organmnist3d-resnet18

## Summary
A 3D ResNet-18 that classifies OrganMNIST3D volumes (liver, kidney-right, kidney-left, femur-right, femur-left, bladder, heart, lung-right, lung-left, spleen, pancreas) from 64³ inputs.
No run is published yet, so there are no results to report.

## Intended use
Research only. Not a medical device. Do not use it to diagnose or treat anyone, or on data
other than OrganMNIST3D volumes.

## Data
- OrganMNIST3D from MedMNIST v2 (Yang et al., Scientific Data 10:41, 2023,
  doi:10.1038/s41597-022-01721-8), the 64³ archive `organmnist3d_64.npz` from Zenodo record
  10519652. Source: LiTS (Bilic et al., arXiv 1901.04056), abdominal CT.
- License: CC BY-NC-SA, inherited from LiTS (its paper, arXiv 1901.04056, section 3.2; no version stated). MedMNIST declares CC BY 4.0. `opendiagnostics publish` withholds example images and weights for this license.
- Split: LiTS's own split, by scan: 115 training and 16 validation scans from the LiTS training set, and 70 test scans from its test set. Train / val / test: 971 / 161 / 610
  volumes (`medmnist` metadata). MedMNIST publishes no patient IDs, so leakage cannot be
  checked.
- Labels: 11 body organs, from bounding-box annotations of the LiTS volumes.

## Training
Config: `config.yaml`, generated by `scripts/medmnist_recipes.py` (its docstring explains the
choices). MONAI ResNet-18 with 3D convolutions trained from scratch, AdamW, cosine schedule,
bf16 mixed precision, EMA weights, batch 16, 100 epochs; sized for a 12 GB GPU. Commit,
hardware, and training time: from the run's `run.json`.

## Limitations and biases
Volumes keep the body's left and right, and several classes are left or right organs (kidney, femur, lung), so training uses no flips and no flip test-time augmentation.
The volumes are 64³ downsamplings, which lose fine detail.

## Reproduce
```bash
uv run opendiagnostics fit -c models/organmnist3d-resnet18/config.yaml
```
